Still Games Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 5, 2026

Still Games does not collect, transmit, sell, rent, or share personal data.

Data Collection

Still Games does not collect any personal information, gameplay data, device identifiers, diagnostics, analytics, location data, contacts, photos, audio recordings, or other user content.

The app does not use accounts, advertising, analytics SDKs, third-party tracking SDKs, or tracking identifiers.

Data Stored on Your Device

Still Games stores a small amount of app data locally on your device so the app can remember your preferences and progress. This includes:

  • Sound, haptic, motion, and appearance settings
  • Whether onboarding has been completed
  • Local game progress, such as completion counts and last-played dates
  • Local journey information, such as total completions and streak status

This data stays on your device and is not sent to us or to any third party.

Deleting Your Data

You can delete the app's local data from within the app:

Settings → Delete all data

You can also remove the app from your device to delete its local app data.

Network Use

Still Games does not make network requests as part of normal app use.

If you open the Privacy Policy link from Settings, your device will open this privacy policy page in your browser.

Children's Privacy

Still Games does not knowingly collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes to This Policy

If Still Games changes in a way that affects privacy, this policy will be updated before those changes are released.

Contact

For privacy or support questions, contact: dt-app-support@proton.me
